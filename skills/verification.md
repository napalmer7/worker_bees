# Skill: Evidence-based verification

Use before reporting that a change, search, measurement, or tool is verified.

1. State the question and artifact revision.
2. Confirm the instrument can observe the target and covers intended scope.
3. Confirm the operation ran and targeted the intended artifact (identify resolved workspace/module when ambiguous).
4. Use known cases or controls when an empty/green result could be vacuous. Controls should share the property that could hide the target.
5. Preserve raw output or a stable reference where practical. Distinguish query failure from zero results.
6. Report verified, not run, inconclusive, or blocked; name what remains unchecked.
7. Corroborate important claims with a different instrument or source where practical. Rerunning one method confirms only its computation.

Do not turn “not observed in this search” into “does not exist.” State the search space.
